Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Selfera is a social app: your posts are visible to everyone on Selfera. This page explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

Selfera is run by Pavol Repiský, IČO 56913982, Slovakia, who is the controller of your personal data. For anything about your data, write to support@selfera.app.

What others can see

  • Your profile (name, username, bio and photo) and your past days (photos, captions and the eras they belong to) can be seen by anyone signed in to Selfera.
  • Today's photos stay hidden from each person until they've photographed one of their own tasks that day. Comments on your day can be read before that.
  • Comments and likes you leave appear with your name.

Don't post anything you wouldn't want other people on Selfera to see. They could also save or screenshot it.

What we collect

When you make an account

  • Your email address and password, stored as a secure hash, or your sign-in details from Apple if you use Sign in with Apple.
  • Your name, username, and, if you add them, a bio and a profile photo.

When you use Selfera

  • The photos you take for your tasks, when you took them, and your captions.
  • The eras you create or join, their tasks, the weekdays and reminder times you pick, and your progress.
  • Your friends and friend requests, comments, likes, which photos you've seen, and your notifications.
  • Your phone's time zone, because each day of an era runs from midnight to midnight where you live.
  • Anyone you block, what you report, and any reports or strikes against your account.

When you join the waitlist on this website

  • Your email address, your invite code, whose invite brought you, and where you came from.
  • If analytics are on, pages viewed and clicks on the waitlist page, measured by PostHog. We identify you there by your invite code, not your email. It doesn't use cookies or store anything in your browser.

What we don't collect

  • Location. The app removes location data (GPS) from your photos before they're uploaded, and it doesn't ask for your location.
  • Contacts. Selfera doesn't read your address book.
  • Advertising data. There are no ads in Selfera. We don't sell your data or track you across other companies' apps.

Your reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. The camera and photo library are used only when you allow them, and only for what you choose to take, pick or save.

Who we share it with

We use these providers to run Selfera. They handle data on our behalf and only for that.

  • Supabase: database, photo storage and sign-in.
  • Vercel: hosting for this website.
  • Resend: sending reports to our moderators by email.
  • PostHog: analytics on the waitlist page only.
  • Google Fonts: this website's typefaces, which your browser loads from Google.
  • Apple: if you use Sign in with Apple.

We may also share information with the police or other authorities when the law requires it, or where someone is in danger. Where a provider handles data outside the EEA, we rely on the safeguards the GDPR allows, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your data, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit that use. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Most of your data you can change or delete in the app; for anything else, email support@selfera.app and we'll answer within a month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Age

Selfera is for people aged 16 and over. If we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
