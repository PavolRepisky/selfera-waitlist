Support

Something not working, or a question? Email support@selfera.app and tell us your username and what happened. We read every message.

Your account

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your email. We'll send you a link to set a new one.

How do I change my name, username or photo?

Go to Profile, then Edit profile.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, Settings, then Delete account. Here's what that removes, and how to ask without the app.

Eras

When does a task count?

Once you've photographed it, and only on the day itself. Each day runs from midnight to midnight where you live.

What happens if I miss a day?

You lose a life. Each era sets how many missed days it allows, and missing more than that ends your run. A rest day, with no tasks due, can't be missed.

Why are Community's posts locked?

They open once you've photographed one task today.

How do I change my reminders?

Go to Profile, Settings, then Reminders. If none arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Selfera in your phone's settings.

Safety

How do I report something?

Tap the ⋯ on a profile, post, story or era and choose Report, or tap Report beside a comment. People review every report, and we aim to act within 24 hours.

How do I block someone?

Tap the ⋯ on their profile, post or story and choose Block. To undo it, go to Profile, Settings, then Blocked.

My account was suspended or banned

If you think that's a mistake, email support@selfera.app and tell us why. See how moderation works.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
