Delete your Selfera account

You can delete your account and its data at any time. It can't be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Selfera and go to Profile.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@selfera.app from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Selfera account”, and include your username. We may ask you to confirm it's you, then delete the account within 30 days and let you know.

What's deleted

  • Your account, email address, name, username, bio and profile photo
  • Your task photos, posts and captions
  • Your comments, likes, friends, blocks, notifications and progress in eras
  • The photos of any era you created

What's kept

  • Eras you created: the name, description and tasks stay for the people in them, without your name attached.
  • Reports: reports and moderation decisions, including the reported content's text, are kept to handle disputes and meet our legal duties. Deleting your account doesn't close a report about it.
  • Backups and service logs: these may hold your data for a short time until our providers' normal cycle removes them.

Screenshots or photos other people saved are outside our control. To be removed from the waitlist, email support@selfera.app. More in the Privacy Policy.
